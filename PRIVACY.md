# Privacy policy — Nila Diha's Ghost Radar

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Nila Diha's Ghost Radar ("the app") is a Halloween prank app for Android and iOS, published by Hariprasad Sudharshan.

**The app collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network access. The Android build does not request the internet permission.

**Camera.** The app asks for the camera so it can look for a doll or toy in front of the phone. Each camera frame is analysed on the phone by an on-device model and then discarded. No frame is stored, uploaded or shared. The camera is only on while the hunt screen is open.

**Vibration and sound.** The app vibrates and plays sounds as part of the prank. Nothing is recorded.

**Children.** The app is a general-audience entertainment app with jump-scare and horror themes. It is not directed at children.
